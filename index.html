Saleh Afroogh

AI & Data Scientist · Technical Project Manager

I am an AI Research Staff at the Information School at UT Austin, where I work on responsible AI/LLM, reasoning, and interpretable AI in LLMs across domains including science, health, design, and risk management. I led an AI-powered initiative, The-Responsible-AI-Initiative, that probes LLMs for hallucination, bias, and harmful outputs, building its safety benchmarks and the end-to-end ML stack, from data pipelines to model evaluation in PyTorch and scikit-learn. Since 2023, I have managed the Responsible AI portfolio within UT Austin’s NSF NRT program, coordinating research priorities across disciplines and helping communicate the program’s work to academic, industry, and community audiences. In this role I connect technical AI research with ethics, governance, and real-world use.

I also direct a cross-national explainable AI (XAI) project with 49 scholars from IBM, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, MIT, Stanford, and Harvard. Before UT, I was Co-PI in the SUNY–IBM AI Research Alliance, designing probabilistic trust frameworks for AI systems.

I have authored and led work published in Nature Human Behaviour, Nature Scientific Reports, AAAI/ACM, ICLR, etc.
